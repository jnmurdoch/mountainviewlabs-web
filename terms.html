About Notifier

Notifier is an event tracking and reminder application developed by Mountainview Labs for personal and experimental use.

Use of the Application

Notifier provides event monitoring, SMS notifications, and optional Google Calendar integration.

The application is provided as-is, without guarantees of availability, accuracy, or uninterrupted operation.

Third-Party Services

Notifier integrates with third-party services, including Google Calendar and an SMS delivery provider. Use of these services is also subject to their applicable terms and policies.

Google Calendar Authorization

Google Calendar integration requires authorization from the Google Account owner. Authorization can be revoked through Google Account settings.

Limitations

Notifier should not be relied upon as the sole mechanism for critical, emergency, medical, financial, or time-sensitive notifications.

Changes

These terms may be updated to reflect changes to the application or its operating requirements.

Contact

Questions can be directed to the application operator through the support contact identified in the Google OAuth consent screen.
